        Teach a sign

        Record yourself signing a word three times. It makes the model match your signing, and you can add words the dataset does not have (for example "want", "where", "go").

        Select Start camera (on the left), then type a word and record.

        1. Sit so your head, shoulders and hands are in view.
        2. After the countdown, sign the word once, start to finish (about 2 seconds).
        3. When you have recorded your words, run finetune.bat (a few minutes). Reload this page.
